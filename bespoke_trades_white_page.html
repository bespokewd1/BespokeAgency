Common questions

Trades business owners ask us...

Straight answers, no runaround.

Do I need to write my own content or provide photos?
Nope. Fill out a short questionnaire (about 5 minutes) and we write everything — page content, service descriptions, and social media captions. For photos we use professional stock images to start, and swap in your real job photos whenever you have them.
I already have a website. Can you replace it?
Yes. We rebuild it from scratch and keep your existing domain name. Nothing goes down during the switch — your old site stays live until the new one is ready.
What do the 5 social media posts look like?
A mix of job spotlights, before/after photos of your work, seasonal promotions, and tips relevant to your trade. All branded to your company. You get to approve every post before it goes live.
Is there a setup fee or long-term contract?
No setup fee and no long-term contract. We're month-to-month. We want to earn your business every single month — if after 90 days it's not working for you, cancel anytime, no penalty.
How long until my site is live?
Usually 2–3 weeks from the time you sign up and fill out the questionnaire. We design first, get your approval, then build and launch.
